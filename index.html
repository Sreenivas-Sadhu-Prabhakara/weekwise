      Private by construction

      “This record never leaves your browser” is enforced, not promised: this page ships a Content-Security-Policy of connect-src 'none', so the browser itself blocks any network send. No accounts, no analytics, no cloud. Your dates, ticks and name live only in this browser’s localStorage — clearing site data erases them. The hospital record / MCP card remains the record of truth.

      There are no reminders or notifications of any kind — the chart only updates when you open it.

      What this app will not do

      • No clinical advice. Your obstetrician/midwife overrides this chart — it only dates the cited published windows.
      • An EDD is an estimate. Only 4.4% of babies come on their due date (Royal Berkshire NHS FT, May 2026) — the app never predicts labour.
      • No replanning. Missed windows show as “passed”; the clinician replans. No reminders either — the chart only updates when opened.
      • Routine singleton schedules only. Multiple, IVF and high-risk pregnancies follow different plans this app does not model, and there are no IVF/conception-date modes or cycle-length adjustments — the honest override is entering your scan EDD.
      • No week-by-week fetal-development editorial (“baby is the size of a mango”) — that content is not citable to a guideline window, so it isn’t here.
      • No symptom checkers, kick counters or vitals logging, and no complication/high-risk pathway content.
